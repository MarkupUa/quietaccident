About Night Pulse

Night Pulse moves closer to a late-night club atmosphere while retaining the cinematic identity of Quiet Accident. Deep low frequencies and a smooth electronic groove form the foundation, creating a darker and more intimate sense of movement.

Ukrainian vocal elements bring a human presence into the electronic arrangement, while subtle melodic echoes suggest a connection to Ukrainian musical tradition.

The track explores another route within the Quiet Accident sound: less focused on the open highway and more on the deep pulse of electronic music after midnight.
