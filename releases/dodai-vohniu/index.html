About Dodai Vohniu

Dodai Vohniu, meaning "Add Fire" in Ukrainian, brings a more forceful rhythmic character to the Quiet Accident catalogue. The track is driven by syncopated electronic movement, strong low frequencies and Ukrainian vocal phrases that become part of the groove.

Rather than using a traditional verse-and-chorus structure as its central identity, Dodai Vohniu emphasizes rhythmic momentum and instrumental energy. Its atmosphere evokes neon-lit streets, motion and rising intensity, making it a distinct entry in the project's night-drive electronic sound.
