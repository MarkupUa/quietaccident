Dark Electronic Music for Night Drives

Quiet Accident is an independent electronic music project exploring the darker side of movement and sound. Drawing on driving techno, dark electronic textures and melodic tension, its tracks combine deep low frequencies, rhythmic energy and cinematic atmosphere. The music is shaped around the feeling of travelling through an empty city, following tunnel lights or crossing a highway long after sunset.

Some releases are built around hypnotic instrumental passages, while others introduce short Ukrainian vocal phrases as part of the rhythm and atmosphere. From darker club-focused tracks to electronic interpretations of Ukrainian melodies, Quiet Accident explores different routes through the same after-dark world.
